# Contributing

Found a bug or have an idea? [Open an issue](../../issues). For a bug, include your macOS version and what Bouncer showed.

Want to fix something? Open a pull request. Keep it to one change, and for anything big, open an issue first.

To build Bouncer, open the project in Xcode and choose your own team under Signing & Capabilities, but don't commit that change.

Contributions are released under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
